Quick workflow — best results
  1. Outline the property — Drag corners/edges until the boundary matches the fence lines.
  2. Square it — Click RECT to square the property boundary (works best with 4 corners).
  3. Select the house — Click Select House, then click-and-drag over the house. Use the rotate handle to align.
  4. Add the shed — Enter shed dimensions, click Add Shed, then drag it into position.
  5. Square the shed — Click SHED on the map to align with the nearest fence line.
  6. Mark shadows (optional) — Click SHDW, then click shadow areas to label them for AI removal.
  7. Generate — Click Generate, then paste the image into Gemini.
  8. Add clearances — If the plan looks good, click Copy clearance prompt and paste into Gemini.
  9. Refine if needed — ↻ Use Gemini's Regenerate until the plan looks right.
